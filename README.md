# Machine Fault Reporting and Maintenance Response System

## Overview

The Machine Fault Reporting and Maintenance Response System is designed to improve communication and coordination between Production and Maintenance departments in manufacturing environments.

The system provides a centralized platform for reporting machine faults, managing maintenance responses, and tracking faults from initial submission through resolution and closure. It aims to improve fault traceability, reduce communication delays, and maintain a historical record of machine faults and maintenance activities.

## Objectives

- Simplify the reporting of machine faults by production personnel.
- Improve communication between Production and Maintenance departments.
- Support the assignment and tracking of maintenance work.
- Provide visibility into fault status and maintenance progress.
- Maintain historical records of machine faults and maintenance responses.
- Support the analysis of machine faults and maintenance activities through reporting.

## Planned Features

- **Machine Fault Reporting** — Submit fault reports with descriptions of the problem and details of the affected machine.
- **Machine Identification** — Identify and track the machines associated with reported faults.
- **Media Attachments** — Include photographs, audio recordings, and videos to help explain reported faults.
- **Fault Notifications** — Notify relevant personnel when faults are reported and when important status changes occur.
- **Fault Review and Assignment** — Allow Maintenance personnel to review reports and assign technicians.
- **Status Tracking** — Track faults through reporting, review, assignment, work in progress, resolution, and closure.
- **Maintenance Response Recording** — Record investigation findings, corrective actions, and repair details.
- **Status Updates** — Keep Production personnel informed about the progress of reported faults.
- **Fault History** — Maintain records of reported faults and their associated maintenance activities.
- **Search and Filtering** — Find reports based on relevant criteria, such as machine, status, or reporting date.
- **Reporting and Analytics** — Provide summaries of machine faults and maintenance activities to support monitoring and decision-making.

## Intended Users

- **Production Personnel** — Report machine faults and follow the progress of submitted reports.
- **Maintenance Personnel** — Review faults, coordinate responses, assign technicians, and record maintenance activities.
- **Maintenance Technicians** — Investigate reported faults, perform repairs, and document corrective actions.
- **Administrators** — Manage system access and relevant configuration, subject to the final requirements.
